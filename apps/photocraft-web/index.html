Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is PSDEdit?

PSDEdit is a browser-based tool designed to help you open and edit PSD documents online.

Is PSDEdit free to use?

Yes. PSDEdit is free to use and does not require an account.

Do I need Photoshop installed?

No. You can access PSDEdit in a supported modern browser without installing Photoshop.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or login is required to get started.

Which files can I open?

The editor supports PSD files. Support for other formats depends on the capabilities of the editor.

Can I use PSDEdit on my phone?

You can open the website on a mobile browser, but editing complex PSD documents is generally easier on a desktop or laptop.

Edit PSD Files Online for Free with EditPSD.net

EditPSD.net is a free online PSD editor that lets you open and work with Photoshop Document (PSD) files directly in your browser. Edit supported designs, review layers, customize text and visual elements where supported, and save or export your work to your computer. No account or paid subscription is required, and EditPSD.net is intended to remain free to use.

PSD is the native document format used by Adobe Photoshop to store layered designs such as flyers, posters, social media graphics, menus and marketing materials. With an online PSD editor, you can access compatible design files without installing Photoshop. Upload a PSD or PSB file, make your edits in the browser, and export your work to your desktop. For the best experience with large documents, use an up-to-date desktop browser such as Google Chrome.
