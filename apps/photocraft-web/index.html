Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is PSDEdit?

PSDEdit is a browser-based tool designed to help you open and edit PSD documents online.

Is PSDEdit free to use?

Yes. PSDEdit is free to use and does not require an account.

Do I need Photoshop installed?

No. You can access PSDEdit in a supported modern browser without installing Photoshop.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or login is required to get started.

Which files can I open?

The editor supports PSD files. Support for other formats depends on the capabilities of the editor.

Can I use PSDEdit on my phone?

You can open the website on a mobile browser, but editing complex PSD documents is generally easier on a desktop or laptop.
